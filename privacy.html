Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jogos Online Gratis ("we", "us") collects when you visit https://jogosgratis.github.io, how it is used, and the choices you have. The website is operated by JogarGratis, who is responsible for your data and can be reached at icopiare@example.com.

Information we collect

  • No account required. You can play every game without registering, so we do not ask for your name, email or password to use the site.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us by email or through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and the content of your message.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records technical information such as IP address, browser type, device, referring page and the pages visited, for security and to keep the site working.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, as described below and in our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To reply to your messages and handle your requests.
  • To display advertising that funds the site.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and invalid traffic.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required by law, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

Games provided by third parties

Games are embedded from external game providers and run inside a frame on our pages. The provider may collect technical data or store information on your device (for example, to save your game progress) under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only with service providers that help us run the site (such as hosting and advertising partners), when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the website.

Data retention

Messages you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them and to keep a record of requests such as copyright or privacy requests. Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR (European Union), the LGPD (Brazil) and the CCPA/CPRA (California) — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, email icopiare@example.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's privacy

Jogos Online Gratis is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the bottom of this page shows when it was last revised.
