Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Jogos Online Gratis and its partners use cookies and similar technologies when you visit the website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They help the site work, remember your choices and measure how it is used. Similar technologies include local storage and pixel tags.

Cookies used on this website

  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense). Google and its partners use cookies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and, with your permission where required, personalize ads based on your interests.
  • Consent cookies. Where a consent banner is shown, a cookie remembers your choice so you are not asked on every page.
  • Game provider cookies. Embedded games are served by third-party providers that may use cookies or local storage, for example to save your progress or settings.

We do not use cookies to identify you by name, and you do not need an account to play.

Managing cookies

  • Use the consent banner, when shown, to accept or reject non-essential cookies and to change your choice later.
  • Opt out of personalized Google ads at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may stop some games from saving progress.

For more details about how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
